A side project · free and open source · for Frigate NVR

Timelapses from your Frigate cameras, made easy

I wanted a simple way to turn a day of camera footage into a one-minute timelapse. That part works. Then I got a little carried away, so it also does live view, a recordings timeline, clips and notifications. It's free, and it stays small.

A whole day in a minute

This is the reason the app exists. Pick the cameras and a time range, say how long the timelapse should be, and Chismoso works out the speed. Frigate does the rendering, several cameras at a time, and the files land in a folder you choose. Plain clips work the same way.

  • Fast mode decodes keyframes only, many times quicker for long ranges.
  • Optionally delete the server copy once it's downloaded.
  • The same engine runs from the command line for scheduled jobs.

Watch live, without the wait

Every camera in a grid, or one camera full size. Chismoso picks the best way to stream on its own: WebRTC through Frigate's go2rtc when it can, MSE when it can't, and snapshots as a last resort, so there's always a picture.

  • Use Frigate's camera groups, or build your own layouts.
  • Substreams keep the grid light; the full view lets you pick the stream.
  • Each tile shows the frame rate you're actually getting.
More about live view

Scroll back through time

Under the grid sits a timeline you can zoom from weeks down to seconds, with alerts in red and detections in amber. Click anywhere and every camera in the layout plays from that moment, in step.

  • Hover an event to see its thumbnail; jump to the previous or next one.
  • Play from −16× (backwards) to 16×, with J K L like a video editor.
  • Select a stretch to play it, save it, or turn it into a timelapse.
More about the timeline

Know when something happens

New Frigate alerts arrive as native notifications, with a snapshot when a person is detected. Choose the cameras, add detections if you like, and set a cooldown so a busy driveway doesn't bury you.

No MQTT broker or cloud service involved: the app listens to Frigate directly while it's running.

Smaller things that came along the way.
