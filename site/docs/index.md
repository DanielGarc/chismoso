Chismoso is a small, fast app for watching and reviewing the cameras on your [Frigate NVR](https://frigate.video) server. It runs on Windows, macOS and Linux, with Android and iOS on the way. These docs cover everything from the first download to scripting timelapses from the command line.

> [!NOTE]
> Chismoso is an independent project. It isn't affiliated with or endorsed by the Frigate project; it simply talks to your Frigate server the same way Frigate's own web interface does.

## How to get around

- The **sidebar** lists every page, grouped by topic. On a phone it's behind the **Menu** button at the top of the page.
- **Search** sits above the sidebar. Press <kbd>/</kbd> anywhere to jump to it, use the arrow keys to pick a result and <kbd>Enter</kbd> to open it.
- Long pages have an **On this page** outline on the right (on wide screens).
- At the bottom of every page there are **Previous** and **Next** links, so you can read the docs front to back.
